Our Story

More Than Music.
A Movement.

Founded in 2025, the school band has rapidly grown into a proud symbol of rhythm, discipline, and school spirit. Led by Band Master Arav Garg, the band showcases passion and teamwork through every performance. With talented members Krish, Chae, Kevin, Kana, Anaya, and Ajay, the ensemble brings energy and dedication to every event. United by a shared love for music, the band has created a strong identity within the school community, inspiring others through creativity and commitment. As a dynamic and growing group, the school band continues to set the stage for excellence and memorable performances in the years to come.

We believe music is a language that transcends boundaries. Every rehearsal, every note, every performance is a step toward building a community bound by creativity, discipline, and the joy of shared expression.

"Music gives a soul to the universe, wings to the mind, flight to the imagination, and life to everything."
— Arav Garg
